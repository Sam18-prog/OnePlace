# Student Guide

Welcome to OnePlace — one place to discover campus events, follow student clubs, and keep track of what's happening this week.

---

## 1. Discovering Events

- **Public Browsing**: You can browse public campus events and clubs immediately without creating an account.
- **Timeframes & Search**: Filter by **Today**, **This Week**, or **Later**, use category shortcuts in the hero, or search by club name, event title, or room number.
- **Optional Campus Tour**: If you are new to OnePlace, click **Quick campus tour** below the hero (or under **Profile → Open Campus Tour**) for a 4-step walkthrough of topics, clubs, and upcoming events. You can skip it at any time.

---

## 2. Signing In, RSVPs & Following Clubs

Sign in with your verified Google account to unlock personalized features:

- **One-Tap RSVP**: Click **Going / RSVP** on any published event to save it to **My Events**. You can cancel your RSVP anytime.
- **Follow Clubs**: Open the **Clubs** tab, explore student organizations, and click **Follow** to keep their upcoming events near the top of your **Discover** feed.
- **Optional Interests**: Pick up to 6 campus event categories in **Hero → Interests** or **Profile** to tailor **Recommended for You**.

---

## 3. Notifications & Preferences

- Open the bell icon or **Notifications** tab to view in-app updates, club activity, and school-wide announcements.
- Visit **Notification Preferences** to toggle specific alert types (24h/1h reminders, followed club activity, announcements) or turn off alerts altogether.
- Browser push notifications are strictly opt-in and only activate if your school enables Web Push on a configured Firebase project.

---

## 4. Privacy & Feedback

- **Private Attendance**: Your RSVPs and followed clubs are private to your account. Other students and club organizers only see aggregate counts—never your name or email on an attendee roster.
- **Share Feedback**: Spot a bug or have an idea? Click **Send Feedback** in the footer or inside **Profile**. Submissions are sent privately to your school administrators and never displayed publicly.
