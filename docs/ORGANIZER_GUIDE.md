# Club Organizer Guide

This guide explains how approved student leaders and club advisors manage clubs and campus events in OnePlace.

---

## 1. Getting Organizer Access

1. Sign in to OnePlace using your verified Google account.
2. Open the **Profile** tab.
3. Expand **Need organizer access? Show account support ID** and click **Copy ID**.
4. Send your Support ID and club name to your School Administrator.
5. Once assigned, refresh the page—**Organizer Studio** will appear in your navigation and Profile.

> Note: Organizers cannot self-assign roles or manage clubs they have not been assigned to.

---

## 2. Organizer Quick Start Checklist

When you first open **Organizer Studio → Overview**, you will see a 5-step Quick Start card:

1. **Club profile reviewed** — Check your club's description, meeting schedule, and room location.
2. **Club branding completed** — Add a concise tagline, meeting time, or optional HTTPS cover/logo image.
3. **First event created** — Draft your next club meeting or campus activity.
4. **First event published** — Publish so students can discover it and RSVP.
5. **Event management & analytics reviewed** — Review RSVP counts, edit/cancel flows, and the **Analytics** tab.

You can hide this card once finished and reopen it anytime under **Settings → Reopen Organizer Quick Start**.

---

## 3. Managing Events (Draft, Publish, Edit, Cancel)

- **Drafts**: Visible only to you and school administrators until published. Use drafts to verify dates, rooms, and descriptions first.
- **Publishing**: Requires an active assigned club and a valid start/end time in your school's canonical timezone.
- **Visibility**:
  - `Public`: Visible to signed-out campus visitors and signed-in students.
  - `School Only`: Visible only to verified active members of your school.
- **Editing**: Keep times, locations, and descriptions up to date; active RSVPs are automatically preserved.
- **Soft Cancellation**: Events are never hard-deleted so students who RSVP'd aren't left confused. Click **Cancel Event**, provide a clear reason (e.g., *"Moved to next Thursday due to assembly"*), and confirm. Cancelled events remain visible with a cancellation notice and stop accepting new RSVPs.

---

## 4. Understanding Privacy-First Analytics

In **Organizer Studio → Analytics**, you can review:

- Total RSVPs, published events, and average RSVPs per event for your assigned clubs.
- Top-performing events and category breakdowns.
- PII-free CSV exports for club officers or faculty advisors.

To protect student privacy, OnePlace shows **aggregate RSVP and follower counts only**—individual student names, emails, and attendee rosters are never exposed.
